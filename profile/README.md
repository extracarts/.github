## A Shopify App that offers merchants extra revenue

Through extra carts...

So why is this relevant?

Merchants are usually good at one thing: **They have a great sourcing knowledge**.

There are normally a few things that bring challenges:

1. ecommerce tech setup: this is what shopify and their agency network is solving
2. Logistics: The area of Amazon and DHL offering their services
3. Marketing: Where Google and Meta want the budget

So what is left?

**True customer upsell and cross sell guidance**

Amazon pioneered this with their: Who bought A also bought B

Extra Carts brings this data driven approach to all Shopify merchants.

**But paid only if it works**
